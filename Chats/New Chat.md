# Banking Explained

Banking refers to the process of managing financial transactions between individuals or organizations. It involves storing, transmitting, and verifying transactions between different parties, typically using a network of financial institutions.

### Types of Banks

There are several types of banks, including:

*   **Commercial Banks**: Primary depository institutions that provide financial services to businesses and individuals.
*   **Investment Banks**: Specialized banks that help clients raise capital for various purposes, such as mergers and acquisitions or initial public offerings (IPOs).
*   **Cooperative Banks**: Member-owned banks that operate under a shared-management structure. [1]

### Key Banking Concepts

Some important banking concepts include:

*   **Deposit Accounts**: Accounts where individuals or businesses deposit money, earning interest.
*   **Loan Accounts**: Accounts where borrowers receive funds to use for various purposes, such as buying a home or starting a business.
*   **Credit Cards**: Debit-type accounts that allow users to make purchases and pay for goods and services.
*   **Money Market Accounts**: Time deposits offered by banks that earn interest on customers' deposits.

### Banking Functions

A bank's primary functions include:

*   **Accepting Deposits**: Receiving funds from depositors in exchange for a promise to return the deposited amount at a later time.
*   **Making Loans**: Providing credit to borrowers, earning interest on the loaned amount.
*   **Managing Risk**: Hedging against market fluctuations and protecting assets through various financial instruments.

### Banking Regulations

Governments worldwide regulate banking activities to ensure stability, security, and fairness. Key regulations include:

*   **Financial Stability Oversight Committee (FSOC)**: A committee established by the US Congress to oversee financial stability.
*   **International Monetary Fund (IMF)**: An international organization that promotes global economic cooperation.
*   **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**: A EU regulation aimed at protecting personal data and privacy.

### Banking Tools

Common banking tools include:

*   **Accounting Software**: Systems used to manage financial transactions, such as QuickBooks or Xero.
*   **Online Banking Platforms**: Digital platforms that allow users to perform various banking tasks remotely.
*   **Mobile Banking Apps**: Mobile apps that enable users to access their bank accounts and conduct basic banking functions on-the-go.
